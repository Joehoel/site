# Career

Career describes the author's confirmed employment roles.

## Language

**Role**:
A position held with an employer during a stated period.
_Avoid_: Job entry

**Career date**:
A calendar date known to a year, month, or day of precision.

**Active role**:
A role whose inclusive period contains the reference date.
_Avoid_: Latest role

**Primary role**:
An explicitly designated role that takes precedence when multiple roles are active.

**Current selection**:
The active role to present, no active role, or an unresolved overlap between active roles.
